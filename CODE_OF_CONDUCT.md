# Community Guidelines

Agentic Blueprints is a community learning project. Help keep participation welcoming, constructive, and focused on improving practical examples.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate, including when disagreeing.
- Give actionable feedback about ideas and code rather than making personal attacks.
- Welcome questions and contributions from people with different experience levels.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and unwelcome personal conduct.
- Respect privacy: do not publish another person's personal information or confidential data.

Maintainers may edit, decline, or remove contributions that do not meet these expectations. To report a conduct concern, contact the repository maintainers privately through GitHub. For a security vulnerability, use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
